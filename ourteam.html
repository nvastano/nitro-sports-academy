Pedro Hernandez

Owner & Head Coach

Pedro brings over 15 years of coaching experience and an elite playing background — Division I baseball at Arkansas State University, five years of professional baseball in Puerto Rico, and professional softball. A certified softball pitching instructor through the PAULY program, he coaches athletes from T-ball through elite competition and currently leads the 12U Bombers softball travel team.

Matt Spann

Pitching Instructor

Matt pitched four years at Columbia Central High School before being drafted in 2010 by the Tampa Bay Rays. He spent seven years in professional baseball — three with the Rays and four with the Washington Nationals. Now he brings pro-level insight to every lesson at Nitro, with an emphasis on fundamentals, arm care, and building confident, resilient pitchers.
