Matt Spann

Pitching Instructor

Matt pitched four years at Columbia Central High School before being drafted in 2010 by the Tampa Bay Rays. He spent seven years in professional baseball — three with the Rays and four with the Washington Nationals. Now he brings pro-level insight to every lesson at Nitro, with an emphasis on fundamentals, arm care, and building confident, resilient pitchers.

Hunter Thomas

Softball Instructor & Game Intelligence Coach

Hunter grew up on the ballfield and played baseball through college, spending those same years coaching youth players — an early sign of a calling he'd fully embrace later in life. He went on to compete in softball for over 10 years at multiple levels, building a deep understanding of the game from both sides of the chalk.

In 2021, Hunter began coaching his daughter and something clicked. He fell back in love with youth development all over again — reminded that the greatest impact a coach can have is helping a young athlete build the right habits and love for the game from the very beginning.

What truly sets Hunter apart is 8 years of umpiring experience. He can teach athletes not just how to play — but how to think the game. Understanding the fine details that most players never notice is the kind of edge that separates good athletes from great ones.

Background
⚾ Youth Baseball — Player & Coach (College Years) 🥎 10+ Years — Competitive Softball, Multiple Levels ⚖️ 8 Years — Certified Umpiring Experience 🥎 Youth & Travel Softball Coach (2021–Present)
"The details most players overlook are the ones that decide games."
