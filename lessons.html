Our Lessons

Whether you're picking up a bat for the first time or preparing for tryouts, our coaching staff has a program designed for you.

What We Offer

Training Programs For Every Player

All lessons are available for both baseball and softball. Sessions are tailored to each player's age, skill level, and goals.

⚾ Baseball & Softball

Private Lessons

One-on-one sessions with one of our certified instructors. The most effective way to improve quickly — your coach focuses 100% of their attention on your mechanics, habits, and goals.

1-on-1 instruction All skill levels All ages welcome Personalized plan
👥 Group Training

Small Group Sessions

Train alongside 2–4 other players of similar age and ability. A cost-effective way to receive quality instruction in a competitive, collaborative environment.

2–4 players Age-grouped Skill-matched Team dynamic
⚡ Pitching Specialist

Pitching Instruction

Work exclusively in our dedicated pitching lane with our pitching coach. We cover mechanics, arm care, velocity development, spin rates, pitch sequencing, and the mental side of pitching.

Dedicated pitching lane Mechanics & velocity Arm care included Baseball & softball
🏏 Hitting Specialist

Hitting & Batting

Deep-dive sessions focused entirely on your swing. We break down your mechanics, use cage work to build muscle memory, and develop a consistent, repeatable approach at the plate.

Swing analysis Tee & live work All cage types Approach & mindset
🌟 Youth Development

Youth Beginner Program

Designed specifically for younger players (ages 5–10) who are new to baseball or softball. We focus on fundamentals in a fun, encouraging, and age-appropriate setting.

Ages 5–10 Fundamentals focus Fun environment Baseball & softball
🏆 Advanced

Advanced Player Sessions

For high school and competitive travel ball players looking to sharpen their edge. Sessions are high-intensity and focused on the specific areas that will make the difference at the next level.

HS & travel level High-intensity Next-level prep College exposure

Hunter Thomas

Softball Instructor & Game Intelligence Coach

Hunter grew up on the ballfield and played baseball through college, spending those same years coaching youth players — an early sign of a calling he'd fully embrace later in life. He went on to compete in softball for over 10 years at multiple levels, building a deep understanding of the game from both sides of the chalk.

In 2021, Hunter began coaching his daughter and something clicked. He fell back in love with youth development all over again — reminded that the greatest impact a coach can have is helping a young athlete build the right habits and love for the game from the very beginning.

What truly sets Hunter apart is 8 years of umpiring experience. He can teach athletes not just how to play — but how to think the game. Understanding the fine details that most players never notice is the kind of edge that separates good athletes from great ones.

Background
⚾ Youth Baseball — Player & Coach (College Years) 🥎 10+ Years — Competitive Softball, Multiple Levels ⚖️ 8 Years — Certified Umpiring Experience 🥎 Youth & Travel Softball Coach (2021–Present)
