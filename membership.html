FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What ages do you work with? +
We work with athletes of all ages — from young beginners (ages 5 and up) all the way through high school and beyond. Our programs are designed to meet players where they are, regardless of age or experience level.
Do you offer softball as well as baseball? +
Absolutely! All of our batting cages are available for both baseball and softball, and we have coaches experienced in both sports. Whether you're a shortstop or a pitcher, we've got you covered.
How do I schedule a private lesson? +
You can reach out through our Contact page or visit our online store at nitrosportsacademy.com to purchase a lesson membership. Once booked, we'll work with you to find a time that fits your schedule.
Is there a contract or long-term commitment required? +
No long-term contracts required. Our plans are month-to-month and flexible — we believe in earning your continued membership through results, not locking you in.
Can I bring my own equipment? +
Yes! You're welcome to bring your own bat, helmet, batting gloves, and other gear. We also have equipment available at the facility if needed.
What are your facility hours? +
Hours vary by season. Contact us or check our Facebook page for the most up-to-date schedule. Private lessons can often be arranged outside standard open hours.
