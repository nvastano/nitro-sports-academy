Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who We Are

Nitro Sports Academy ("we," "us," or "our") operates at 3710 John Lunn Rd, Suite 2, Spring Hill, TN 37174. We offer batting cage rentals, baseball and softball instruction, and membership programs. Questions about this policy may be directed to coach.pedro.tn@gmail.com or by calling 615-870-8077.

2. Information We Collect

When you book a cage or sign up for a membership on our website, we collect the following information you provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Payment information (processed securely by Square — we do not store card data)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Confirm and manage your cage bookings
  • Send booking confirmation emails and text messages
  • Manage your membership account
  • Contact you regarding your reservation or membership

4. Text Message Communications

By providing your phone number and submitting a cage booking on our website, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Nitro Sports Academy. These messages include booking confirmations and reservation details.

  • Message frequency: You will receive one text message per booking confirmation. Occasional operational messages may also be sent.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details.
  • To opt out: Reply STOP to any text message you receive from us. You will receive a one-time confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
  • For help: Reply HELP or contact us at 615-870-8077.

We do not share, sell, or rent your mobile phone number to any third party for marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to send communications directly related to your bookings with Nitro Sports Academy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with service providers that help us operate our business (such as Square for payment processing, Google for calendar integration, and Twilio for text messaging), solely for the purpose of delivering services to you. These providers are contractually required to protect your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and maintain accurate records. You may request deletion of your information by contacting us at coach.pedro.tn@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website or services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
