Built to Train

Where Reps Get Made

Nitro Sports Academy is a fully enclosed, climate-controlled indoor training facility in Spring Hill, Tennessee. We built this space from the ground up with one purpose in mind: to give every athlete — from the youngest beginner to the most competitive high schooler — a place to develop their game every single day.

The facility features three full-length batting cages with pro-grade artificial turf, a dedicated pitching lane, proper home plate setups, and a full inventory of training equipment including pitching machines, ball carts, and radar guns. Whether you're here for a private lesson or just to put in your own reps, everything you need is ready to go.

No excuses. No off-season. Just reps.

What's Inside

Everything You Need to Get Better

⚾ Baseball & Softball

3 Full-Length Batting Cages

Three side-by-side batting cages, each with pro-grade artificial turf, a regulation home plate mat, and batter's box markings. Cages are set up for both baseball and softball use — easily configurable for tee work, front toss, or machine pitching at any speed.

🎯 Pitching & Training Tools

Pro Training Equipment

We invest in the right tools — pitching machines, ball carts stocked with baseballs and softballs, radar guns, pitching targets, and training aids. Every piece of equipment is maintained and ready so athletes can focus entirely on their development.

⚡ Pitching Specialist Area

Dedicated Pitching Lane

Our separate pitching lane gives pitchers a dedicated space to work on mechanics, release point, and velocity development away from hitters. With proper mound distance and high-quality flooring, it's the ideal environment for serious pitching instruction at any level.

The Full Picture

Three Cages. One Mission.

Looking down the line at all three cages, you see exactly what Nitro is about — a clean, well-lit, professional indoor space where multiple athletes can train simultaneously without getting in each other's way.

Every cage has its own ball cart, equipment storage, and setup ready for use. Members can walk in, grab a slot, and get straight to work.

  • High-intensity LED lighting throughout — no dark corners, no shadows
  • Turf surface designed for comfort and ball response
  • All 3 cages available for baseball and softball
  • Ball carts fully stocked at each station
  • Separate pitching lane for pitchers to work independently
  • Climate-controlled — comfortable training in any season
  • Ample space between cages for coaches to work alongside hitters
